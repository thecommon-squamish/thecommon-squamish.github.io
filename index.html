Get to know The Common

Good work happens around good people.

You can get your head down here. You can also have lunch with someone who knows the town, ask the person at the next desk for a second opinion, or hear what a neighbour is building.

The Common makes those connections easier with shared spaces and a community lunch every Thursday. Peter opened it on Cleveland Avenue in 2017 to give independent workers a place to belong as well as a place to work.

Why The Common began

Since 2017

The people shaped the place.

Peter's first note to members was a promise to bring people together and listen. When members said they worked across Squamish, Whistler, and Vancouver, that feedback led to a shared Sea to Sky pass. The Common also explored ways for local groups and people with useful skills to take part.

Those older programs tell the story behind the space. Today's booking choices are above; the history has a home of its own.
