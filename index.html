Get to know The Common

Good work happens around good people.

You can get your head down here. You can also have lunch with someone who knows the town, ask the person at the next desk for a second opinion, or hear what a neighbour is building.

We make those connections easier with shared spaces and a community lunch every Thursday. Desks, quiet rooms, a kitchen and a courtyard give you room to focus and room to meet someone new.

Why The Common began

Since 2017

The people shaped the place.

The Common has grown with the people who work here. Over the years, their ideas shaped the desks, meeting spaces, shared meals and ways for local groups to gather.

Those older programs tell the story behind the space. Today's booking choices are above; the history has a home of its own.
