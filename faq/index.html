Good to know before your first day.

How to get in, where to take a call, whether your dog can come, and the other things people ask us.

38016 Cleveland Avenue.

We're in downtown Squamish. You can see current prices and availability when you book online.

Book a roomBook a desk
How do I book a desk or room?

Pick a desk or one of our three bookable rooms here. The booking button takes you to our booking system, where you can see what is free, choose your time, and pay or send a request.

Can I book an office for Friday?

Yes, if a bookable room is free. The Micro Office is for one person, the Trailer seats up to three, and the Small Conference Room seats up to six. Open the room you want, choose Friday in the booking calendar, and it will show the available times. Monthly private offices are a separate rental.

How do I get into the building?

Look for our welcome email after your booking is confirmed. It has the details for finding us and getting through the door. Some bookings need approval first. If the email has not arrived, get in touch before you come down.

Can I take a call at a hot desk?

A quick, quiet call is fine. For a conversation that needs more time or privacy, use the indoor phone booth if it is free, or book a room. We all share the main workspace, so please keep it comfortable for the next person.

Is the phone booth bookable?

The indoor phone booth is for short, unscheduled calls. The Micro Office, Trailer, and Small Conference Room are the spaces you can reserve online.

Can I bring my dog?

We love dogs. Please check with us before bringing yours, keep them settled beside your desk, and clean up after them. The courtyard has artificial turf, so please check it before you leave too.

Is there somewhere to eat or take a break?

Yes. Bring your lunch, store it in the kitchen, and take a seat at the communal table. When the weather is good, the courtyard is there too.

Where do I find my bookings and invoices?

Your reservations, invoices, and space guides are available in The Common’s member portal after you create a booking.
