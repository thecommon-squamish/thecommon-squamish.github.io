A pass built around how members moved.

A short chapter from The Common's early days in Squamish.

From the original March 2018 announcement

The team opened The Common knowing that a Squamish coworking space would have to listen to its own members. They asked what people needed. Founding members described workdays that often took them to Whistler and Vancouver to see clients, attend events, and meet friends.

The response was the Sea to Sky Common Pass, a shared membership announced with Space in Whistler and L’Atelier in Vancouver. At the time, members could use all three spaces as part of one plan. It was a concrete example of a community shaping the service offered to it.

The 2018 partnership is part of The Common's history, not a current membership benefit. Today's plans and booking details are on the membership page.
