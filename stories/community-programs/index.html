Making room for other people's ideas.

The original Common Capital, community grant, and venue sponsorship programs grew from a simple question: who could do more with this space if cost were less of a barrier?

Peter built The Common to make stronger connections between people and organizations in Squamish. The programs below were ways to put that idea to work. They are described here as part of The Common's history. Funding, discounts, and free venue use are not confirmed current offers.

Desk space

Common Capital

This program drew on the idea of social capital that inspired The Common. A person with time, useful skills, or good connections could contribute to the community in exchange for more accessible desk space. Peter especially wanted to hear from people working on innovation, environmental sustainability, Indigenous equity, human rights, and community building.

Local groups

Community grants

The Common worked with Community Futures to help Squamish groups run meetups and events around small business, entrepreneurship, technology, outdoor recreation, and the green economy. The original grant page described support for venue rental, marketing, and promotion, with funding of up to $1,000 at that time.

A place to gather

Venue sponsorship

Peter also wanted local charities and community groups to be able to meet, fundraise, and show films without a venue bill stopping the idea. The original program described nonprofit pricing, free rentals for some community groups, and a profit share option for some events.

Tell us what you're planning.

These historical programs explain the spirit behind the space. If you're organizing something for Squamish, send us the details and we'll tell you what is possible now.
